# Purpose

This document defines the architecture of the Income Distribution and Payout system within the BestWayGrow Financial Architecture.

The Income Distribution Architecture manages the complete lifecycle of business-generated income, including income calculation, validation, distribution, wallet crediting, payout eligibility, TDS deduction, approval, and final settlement.

The system ensures that every income movement is transparent, traceable, rule-based, and permanently recorded through enterprise financial infrastructure.

---

# Objectives

The Income and Payout Architecture is designed to:

- Calculate eligible income
- Apply business rules
- Validate income qualification
- Distribute income correctly
- Maintain income transparency
- Manage payout processing
- Apply taxation rules
- Maintain complete audit records

# Income Validation

Before income distribution, the system validates:

- User Status
- PIN Status
- Business Qualification
- Income Rules
- Rank Eligibility
- Policy Conditions
- Transaction Validity
- Fraud Checks

# Income Wallet

The Income Wallet stores approved income generated by business activities.

Functions:

- Income Credit
- Income History
- Income Classification
- Income Verification
- Income Reporting

Income Wallet records:

- Source Event
- Income Type
- Amount
- Qualification Reference
- Timestamp
- Ledger Reference

---

# Hold Wallet

The Hold Wallet stores income requiring additional validation.

Functions:

- Pending Verification
- Pending Settlement
- Compliance Review
- Security Review

Funds remain protected until approval.
